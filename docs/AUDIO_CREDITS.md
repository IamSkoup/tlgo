# Audio samples and permissions

Every bundled audio clip is from a published **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)** sound collection. They may be redistributed and used in a game. Credits are included for provenance even where the license does not require them. No Disney or GTA audio is used.

| Files in `client/audio/` | Original collection and creator | Original download |
|---|---|---|
| `grass1..3.ogg`, `stone1..2.ogg`, `sand1..2.ogg`, `landing.ogg`, `punch1..2.ogg` | **Impact Sounds**, Kenney. The sand cues are pitched-down carpet samples, and the stone cues are concrete, intentionally repurposed as terrain foley. | [Official Kenney page](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), redistributed via [Material Impacts & Footsteps soundbank](https://instrictlogic.com/AssetLibrary/sounds/banks/material-impacts-footsteps-kenney-2362a2f7/) |
| `bite.ogg`, `reward.ogg` | **RPG Audio**, Kenney | [Official Kenney page](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), redistributed via [RPG Foley soundbank](https://instrictlogic.com/AssetLibrary/sounds/banks/rpg-foley-equipment-doors-kenney-35e3d0a1/) |
| `mud1..2.ogg`, `hit1..2.ogg`, `jump.ogg`, `dodge.ogg`, `collect.ogg`, `wind.ogg`, `savanna.ogg`, `water.ogg`, `thunder.ogg` | **100 CC0 SFX / Foley, Weather & Environmental Loops**, rubberduck | [Collection](https://instrictlogic.com/AssetLibrary/sounds/banks/foley-weather-environmental-loops-rubberduck-2dbd2ff0/) |
| `chase.ogg` | **Chase**, wipics, CC0 | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/chase-1) |
| `tension.ogg` | **Tension Theme**, Umplix, CC0. Converted from WAV to Ogg Vorbis for web playback. | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/tension-theme) |
| `battle.ogg` | **The Final Battle**, skrjablin, CC0 | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/the-final-battle) |

Audio is streamed or loaded from files stored in the project, not embedded from third-party hotlinks. The app uses Web Audio for volume groups, transitions, playback-rate variation and attenuated environmental cues. Some material substitutions (sand/carpet and rock/concrete) are compromises until dedicated recordings are created.
